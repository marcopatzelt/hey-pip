Questionnaire: your sick leave

Before you start

This questionnaire helps us to pay your sick pay on time. It takes about five minutes. Please have your sick note and the name of your employer at hand. You can stop at any time; nothing is saved until you press Submit.

We ask for the first day of your illness exactly as it is written on the sick note from your doctor. If you have several sick notes for the same illness, give the first day of the first note. Your employer needs to know that you are ill, but not why; we never tell your employer your diagnosis.

If you are still unable to work, please send us every follow-up sick note as soon as you have it. Questions about this form? Our service team answers them on weekdays. All information is protected by data protection law and is used only to handle your sick leave.
