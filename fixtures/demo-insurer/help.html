Help and FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Card locked?

Call 0800 555 0100 (invented) or write to us.

Our service team is there Monday to Friday from 8 to 18 o'clock. A new card arrives within about a week.

Forgot your password?

Your account is locked after 3 wrong passwords. It opens again by itself after 30 minutes, or we send you a new start letter by post.

Where do I send my sick note?

Use the upload on our contact page, or send it by post. Please keep the original.
